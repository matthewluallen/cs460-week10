connecting…
On a laptop? Scan with a phone to open this page there. Then Connect and Share the sensors you want. The M5StampLC subscribes to every one of them and keeps each as a network-only phone point with its own 8 s freshness timer (Modbus registers 140–165). A ladder reads a point only if its project binds it to a word input (phone_<point>, or phone_<point>_fresh for the 1/0 fresh flag). Phone points never drive the wired inputs IN1–IN8.
You are the sensor — and the attacker. Share a sensor to feed the PLC live. Then use its two controls: Transmit (turn it off and the PLC loses the reading — an availability attack) and Override (send any value you choose while the plant believes it — an integrity / false-data-injection attack). Watch what the logic does. Each shared sensor gets a 60 s trend: what the phone reads, what was actually sent, and where the point goes stale (8 s after its last message, timed by this phone; the twin's clock can run slower than real time, so the twin may flag it later).

How often each shared sensor sends its newest reading (Motion sends accel and gyro, each at this rate). The page reads the sensor much faster; the trend graph shows both. BTN presses and Transmit ON/OFF act at once. The PLC marks a point stale 8 s after its last message, so a rate slower than one message every 8 s would make points go stale.

Reconstructed, not sampled. QEMU models I²C at the transaction level, so these are decoded bus events placed on the scan clock, not analog SDA/SCL edges. The same transactions export to a VCD that opens in PulseView (bus_to_vcd.py → the real I²C decoder annotates Start/Address/Data/ACK/Stop). Freeze & force mirrors the live control plane: QMP stop → qom-set /machine/soc/aw9523 di=0xFF → cont — change a register while time is stopped, then watch the next scan react.
